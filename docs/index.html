◆House-Hacking, Plainly

The idea: you buy a 2–4 unit building, live in one unit, and rent the others. Their rent covers most — sometimes all — of your mortgage. You live cheaper than renting while building equity.

Why 3.5% down works: FHA loans let you in with 3.5% down, but FHA demands you live there at least a year — which is exactly what a house-hack is. The catch is mortgage insurance (PMI), which this analyzer prices in automatically.

The honest math: vacancy happens and roofs leak, so this tool haircuts your rental income for both before declaring victory. If the verdict still beats your rent, the hack is real.
